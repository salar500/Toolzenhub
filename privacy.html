Privacy Policy

This page explains how ToolZen Hub approaches privacy and information collected through the website.

Last updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

ToolZen Hub respects your privacy and aims to provide a simple and transparent website experience. This Privacy Policy describes the general approach to information that may be collected when you use our website.

2. Information You Provide

Some features may allow you to voluntarily provide information, such as when contacting us or subscribing to a newsletter.

We only request information that is relevant to the feature being used.

3. Calculator Information

Many ToolZen Hub calculators operate directly in your browser. Information entered into a calculator may be used to produce the requested result.

Users should avoid entering unnecessary sensitive or confidential information into calculators or forms.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

ToolZen Hub may use cookies or similar technologies where necessary to support website functionality, understand website usage or improve the user experience.

5. Analytics

We may use analytics or similar services to understand general website usage, such as which pages are visited and how visitors interact with the website.

6. Third-Party Services

ToolZen Hub may use third-party services for hosting, analytics, advertising, forms, email or other website functionality.

These services may have their own privacy policies and terms.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information associated with the website. However, no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

ToolZen Hub is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time as the website and its services develop. The updated version will be posted on this page.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please visit our Contact Us page.
