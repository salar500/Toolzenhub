# 13. Articles and content opportunities

**Audit first:** there are **no Time Tools articles** (`../09-ARTICLES-AND-CONTENT-STRATEGY.md`: 34 published articles, all in loans, investment, business, tax and math). Date Difference's spec explicitly records that a future article on business days versus calendar days could be worthwhile (spec 11). No article may be written in this phase, and none is required: rulebook 6.1 says no article beats a thin one. Related-article display on Time Tools is suppressed today (module flags), so articles would not show until the navigation pilot changes that.
