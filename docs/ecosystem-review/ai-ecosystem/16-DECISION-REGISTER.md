# 16. Final product decision register

Owner instruction, 2026-11-16 (master execution directive): Claude resolves routine product and design decisions from the evidence, records them with their rationale and rejected alternatives, and does not stop for D1-D7. Each decision below is **final for this programme** (owner delegation) and is recorded in the rulebook where it is durable policy (rule 4.11), in the same phase as the code.

Where this register differs from `06` to `15`, **this register and `17-FINAL-DESIGN.md` win**; the earlier documents are kept as the research trail.
